      Key Features
      LED Projector Headlamps
      Boost your driving confidence and safety in poor weather with the powerful LED Head Lamps that deliver crystal clear brightness. 
      Multi Colour Mood Lighting
      Enhance your mood during your drives with Multi Colour Mood Lighting and uplift the ambience on the go.
      Multiple Voice Assistants (Native,Siri,Google Assistant)™
      Virtual convenience redefined. Communicate with your virtual assistant in more than 6 languages and 250+ commands to get everything done.
      Cinematic Experience by JBL™ Audio Modes
      Different music genres deserve different sound modes. Choose a musical mode that suits the mood of your journey.
      Serenity Screen Rear Sunshade
      Cool comfort, instant privacy. Serenity Screen Rear sunshades ensure a peaceful, exclusive cabin for every journey.
      Steering Mounted Controls
      All the important controls mounted on the steering, so you stay focused on the road ahead.
      Rear Arm-Rest with easysip cup docks
      Cafe like experience brought to you with a cushioned armrest and a convenient cup-docks for your favourite beverage.
      Wireless Smartphone Charger
      Keep your phone powered on the go without the hassle of cables.
      iRA connected car technology
      Stay more connected with your CURVV and in greater control, even when you're away from it.
      6 Airbags
      Comprehensive safety of 6 Airbags for you & your loved ones in every drive.
      Breathe IQ Purifier with AQI Display
      Enter the Oasis of Pure and germ free air along with an AQI Display that keeps you informed of the air quality in real time.
      Smart Key with Push-Button Start (PEPS)
      Keyless entry to your car brings ease and innovation together for improved safety and a stress-free driving experience.
      4 spoke Illuminated Digital Steering Wheel
      Buttery smooth ergonomics elevated with Illuminated logo. Each drive will make your heart swell with pride.
      Grand Centre Console with Leatherette Armrest
      Makes your cabin more functional and enjoyable for you and your loved ones.
